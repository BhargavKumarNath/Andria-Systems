# Manager DNA Archetypes

BASED on HDBSCAN clustering of HHI, Put/Call Ratios, Turnout, and AUM, we map the 14.9K universe to:

1. **Conviction Activists (High HHI, Mid AUM):** Highly concentrated portfolios (top 5 holdings > 60%). e.g., Pershing Square, Elliott. *Alpha Profile:* High idiosyncratic risk, strong signal generators.
2. **Index Huggers (Low HHI, Massive AUM):** Highly diversified, mimics S&P 500. e.g., Vanguard, BlackRock. *Alpha Profile:* Zero signal. Filter out.
3. **Macro Tourists (High Put Ratio, High Turnover):** Heavy derivatives use, tail-risk hedgers. e.g., Universa, Bridgewater. *Alpha Profile:* Signal valuable only during 'Recession Fear' regimes.
4. **Quant Clones (Mid HHI, High Turnover):** Statistical arbitrage, factor followers. e.g., Renaissance, Two Sigma. *Alpha Profile:* Crowding risk. Strong fade signals.
5. **Defensive Allocators (Mid HHI, Low Put):** Long-only, buy-and-hold sector specialists (Utilities, Staples).
